About

Curious about the whole stack.

My goal is to grow into ML systems research: understanding models deeply, then making them faster, cheaper, and reliable enough to matter outside a notebook.

I learn best by rebuilding the mechanism, testing it, and writing down the part that finally made it click.

That approach has taken me from a scalar automatic-differentiation engine to character language models, recurrent networks, convolutional architectures, a decoder-only Transformer, and a Linux process tracer built around ptrace.

These notes are not polished claims of mastery. They are a record of implementation: the equations, shapes, failure cases, debugging decisions, and connections I want to remember.
